`boot/550c.html` in this package is an adaptation of the source page's HTML, CSS
and JavaScript. The logo-drawing opening (the SVG 550C mark and its writing
animation) is omitted; the terminal takeover that follows is kept, with a small
host handshake for the DSH iframe overlay (completion message, click/Esc skip,
and a preview marker).
